Discover apps with Luma Store, an Android app store that brings the Luma Store catalog and optional F-Droid repositories together.

BROWSE AND SEARCH
Explore apps by category or search by name, description and package name. Open an app's details to see its version, source and available information.

INSTALL AND UPDATE
Download APKs and confirm installation through Android's installer. Compare catalog versions with installed apps, install available updates or open apps already on your device.

CHOOSE YOUR SOURCES
The Luma Store catalog is enabled by default. Enable optional sources such as Freetime F-Droid, F-Droid and IzzyOnDroid in the Sources tab. You can also add, edit and remove custom repository URLs using the supported index-v1.json format.

App availability and metadata depend on the enabled repositories. Previously loaded catalog information is cached for later browsing.

DEVELOPER AREA
Sign in with GitHub or GitLab to view submissions associated with your account, review feedback and notifications. Mark notifications as read and follow the status of your existing submissions.

REQUIREMENTS
Android 7.0 or newer. An internet connection is needed to refresh catalogs, download apps and use developer services. Android may ask you to allow Luma Store to install apps.

PROJECT STATUS
Luma Store is under active development. Google Play catalog loading and creating new submissions inside the Android app are not currently implemented.
